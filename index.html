📖 Kaise Use Karein (Hindi/Hinglish Guide)

  1. Business Type chuno — jaise Freelancer, E-commerce, Agency, Retail, ya SaaS. Isse tool ke pass India-based ideal benchmark % aa jaate hain.
  2. Apni Monthly Revenue (₹ me) daalo — yeh total income hai, expenses se pehle.
  3. Har category me apna monthly kharcha bharo — Rent, Salaries, Marketing, Software, Travel, Utilities, Supplies, EMI/Loan, aur Misc.
  4. "Analyze Expenses" button dabao. Tool turant calculate karega:
    • Aapka Expense Health Score (0-100)
    • Kaunsi categories overspend ho rahi hain
    • Har category me kitna bacha sakte ho (monthly + yearly)
  5. Jo categories laal/orange bar me dikhengi, unke neeche actionable tips milengi — jaise "unused subscriptions cancel karo" ya "vendor renegotiate karo".
  6. Agar aur deep, personalized advice chahiye to Claude API Key daal kar "Get AI-Written Savings Plan" dabao — yeh ek detailed written plan degा.
  7. Apna data "Save This Scenario" se save kar sakte ho (e.g. month-wise comparison ke liye) — sab kuch aapke browser me hi rehta hai, kahi upload nahi hota.
  8. Yeh tool apne clients ko consulting service ke roop me bhi dikha sakte ho — unka expense audit karke report present karo, upsell opportunity banti hai.
